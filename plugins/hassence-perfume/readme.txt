Turns Hassence into a perfume-only store with a black & gold storefront, Men / Women / Unisex categories and a perfume home page.

== Description ==

This plugin keeps your current Hassence theme, logo, fonts and WooCommerce setup. It adds:

* A perfume home page (flash sale banner, hero, shop by gender, best sellers, new arrivals, oud & attar, gift sets, how to order, WhatsApp help).
* A prominent black & gold flash-sale banner with a live countdown and a direct link to products on sale.
* Black & gold styling layered over the existing theme.
* Product categories: Men's Perfume, Women's Perfume, Unisex Perfume, Oud & Attar, Gift Sets.
* Product tags: Best Sellers, New Arrivals.
* A safe cleanup screen for the old clothing, furniture, toys and padel products.
* A one-click button to deactivate Dokan Lite.

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin.
2. Choose `hassence-perfume-2.1.0.zip` and click Install Now.
3. Click Activate.
4. Open the new **Perfume Mode** menu in the WordPress sidebar.
5. Click **Create categories & organise perfumes**.
6. Click **Export CSV backup** before removing any products.
7. Use **Hide them** (reversible), **Move to Trash**, or **Delete permanently** (type DELETE).
8. Click **Deactivate Dokan Lite**.
9. Clear the LiteSpeed cache and open the homepage.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Will this delete my products automatically? =
No. Only the "Delete permanently" button removes products, and it requires you to type DELETE first. The plugin never deletes products on activation.

= Does it work with my current theme? =
Yes. It uses the active Hassence theme's header, footer and product cards. If you change the theme, the home page template may need adjusting.

= Can I undo the clean-up? =
Hiding and moving to Trash are reversible with the "Restore hidden / trashed products" button. Permanent deletion cannot be undone, so export the CSV first.

= Will new perfumes be deleted? =
No. Products in a perfume category, products with a perfume keyword in the name, and products flagged as perfumes are always protected.
